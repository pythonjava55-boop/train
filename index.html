About Project

Hackathon-built railway safety intelligence

RailGuard AI is a futuristic railway safety solution designed to support Indian Railways-inspired infrastructure with computer vision, predictive analytics, and real-time monitoring. Built for a hackathon, the project demonstrates how AI can identify dangerous track conditions, prioritize maintenance, and give operations teams a fast, professional dashboard for safer decisions.
